Digital Documentation Workflow

Each object is represented through a consistent record to support comparison and preliminary interpretation.

01 · DocumentRecord the object's name, material, date, findspot and museum when stated in the source.
02 · ClassifyGroup the object by archaeological category and chronological period.
03 · CompareReview similarities in material, form, decoration and context.
04 · InterpretPresent a cautious interpretation based on the description available in the course handout.

About MADA Digital Archaeology

MADA Digital Archaeology is an original academic digital archaeology project created by Mariam Abdelrahman (مريم عبد الرحمن), a Level One student at the Faculty of Archaeology, Ain Shams University, designed to support the documentation, classification and interpretation of archaeological finds from ancient Mesopotamia. It demonstrates how structured records and searchable collections can make archaeological information easier to explore and compare.

Source note: The object names, periods, findspots and museum attributions in this version were transcribed or summarized from the supplied course handout, معالم تاريخ وآثار الشرق الأدنى القديم (العراق). If the handout does not name a museum, the record says “Not specified in the handout”. The catalogue is an educational index, not a live inventory or confirmation of present-day display.
